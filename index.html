Read down, then play across.

  1. The fretboard sits above the exact place its chord changes. Low E is on the left. 1 = index, 2 = middle, 3 = ring, 4 = little finger.
  2. Read the chord and lyric together. The diagram, chord label, and lyric position share the same vertical anchor.
  3. Follow the strumming beneath that phrase. Count 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &. Dots mean keep moving without striking the strings.
  4. Beginner mode hides or shows the added guides. Focus hides controls. The visual guide follows a demonstration pattern; it does not play audio or track your guitar.

Customize / print opens the sheet controls. Change text size and line spacing, or choose fingerings and strumming separately. Use Print / Save PDF to print your current sheet or save it from the browser’s print window.

Click or tap a chord, diagram, strumming beat, or lyric position to choose where the highlight goes. Chord buttons also work with Tab and Enter or Space. Pausing keeps the highlight; Play continues from that position. Clear highlight pauses and removes the highlight while keeping your place.

Space: play/pause · B: Beginner mode · F: focus · Esc: exit focus. Shortcuts stay inactive while typing.

To load the full song, paste your own chord-and-lyric text into the editor. Diagrams are available for Am, G, C, and Fmaj7; other chords keep their labels.
